Phase 6: *"Pre-registered retirement tests, by the method of the regime test, for any rule
proposed to move allocation rather than merely inform … The DIAGNOSTIC gate on the

**Status: registration only. No fixed-income retirement computation has been run. Nothing
below may change once a run begins; a change requires a new registration with a stated reason
and both results reported.** Until a contestant passes its paired criterion, no fixed-income
read drives sizing; the rates-regime state stays labeled DIAGNOSTIC and no code path reads it
for sizing.

## A. Provenance of the hypotheses

Each rule below is a candidate the module already displays as a descriptive read (the
rates-regime state, the yield-per-duration carry metric, the credit state). Registering them
as timing rules is a claim that acting on the state beats a static allocation — a claim
generated by looking at the same history the states are computed on. These are therefore
post-hoc hypotheses tested on the history that suggested them; a pass on this window is
evidence of consistency, not an out-of-sample confirmation. This limitation is registered
here and must appear in any results report. The discriminating-power fix established for the
regime test (the paired difference on common resampled paths, which cancels the market-path
variance shared by every contestant — `reports/c3_registration_v2.md` §C) is the statistic;
a margin-vs-own-interval test has no discriminating power and is not used.

## B. Contestants

Sleeve total-return series come from `data/source/bond_sleeves.parquet` (adjusted close,
dividends reinvested). Yields, the curve and spreads are read **point-in-time** from the
vintage store (`fred_indicators_pit.parquet` / `fred_derived_pit.parquet`); the decision is
taken on the as-published series, exactly as the regime test takes it on `R_full`. Signals
use information through session t−1. Monthly rebalance on the first session of each month.

**Test 1 — the rates-regime state as a duration-timing rule.**
- **Challenger:** hold long duration (TLT) when the rates-regime state is `duration`, cash
  (SHV) when `defense`, and an intermediate ladder (IEF) otherwise; exposure mapped through
  the tier cash ranges as in C3 B7.
- **Comparators (two):** (a) a **static duration** allocation (a constant IEF holding at the
  challenger's average duration); (b) a **constant-maturity ladder** (equal weight SHY/IEF/TLT
  rebalanced monthly).

**Test 2 — a carry rule.**
- **Challenger:** hold the sleeve with the highest yield-per-unit-of-duration among the
  Treasury ladder (SHV/SHY/IEF/TLT/GOVT), measured through t−1.
- **Comparator:** **equal weight** across that ladder, rebalanced monthly.

**Test 3 — a credit-timing rule.**
- **Challenger:** add high yield (HYG) when the HY credit state is `wide` or `stressed`,
  reduce to IG (LQD) when `tight`; hold a blend otherwise.
- **Comparator:** a **static credit** allocation (a constant 50/50 HYG/LQD blend at the
  challenger's average credit weight).

## C. Metrics, uncertainty, costs

Maximum drawdown; annualized return; return per unit of volatility; turnover (one-way,
annual); drawdown reduction relative to the comparator. **Costs:** bond-ETF bid-ask spreads
charged at **one third of quoted width** on NAV-weight turnover — narrow for Treasuries
(SHV/SHY/IEF/TLT/GOVT ≈ 1 bp one-third-width), wider for high yield and EM (HYG/JNK/EMB ≈ 3-5
bp one-third-width); the exact per-sleeve widths are frozen in the run script's cost table
when the run is ordered. **Uncertainty:** circular block bootstrap of the daily net return
series of all strategies **jointly** — 60-session blocks, 1,000 resamples, seed 20260916, 90%
intervals (5th-95th percentiles) on every metric except turnover; **paired differences on the
same resampled paths**. Point-in-time yields and spreads from the vintage store throughout;
discriminating power is established by the paired statistic and is checked (reported) before
any adoption.

## D. Decision rule (the paired criterion)

A challenger **dominates** and may be adopted into sizing only if, against **each** of its
comparators, both hold:
1. the 90% interval of the paired difference (challenger − comparator) in after-cost return
   per unit of volatility lies entirely above zero;
2. the challenger's drawdown reduction relative to the comparator is at least zero (point
   estimate).

Any failure = the challenger is not adopted; the corresponding read keeps its role as a
descriptive monitor. **The DIAGNOSTIC gate on the rates-regime state (Test 1) lifts only on a
pass of Test 1 against both comparators, and adoption into live sizing is a separate written
decision** — a passing test plus Werner Hernani-Limarino's written authorization, after which
the label and a sizing consumer are added. A pass is reported with the post-hoc caveat of
section A.
