# C5 registration — Regime index plus volatility targeting versus each alone (registered, NOT run)

Registered 2026-09-09 under the Decision Memo of 9 September 2026, section 4: *"The context runs suggest a hypothesis: volatility targeting captured most of the 2008 protection, and the regime layer's advantage is concentrated in the medium drawdowns of the 2010s, so a combination may dominate both. That is a new claim generated by looking at results. It is registered as C5, a pre-registered test of regime-plus-vol-targeting against each alone, with the paired criterion, and it is not implemented in sizing until it passes."* Order item 4: *"Register C5 ... as a registration document only. Do not run it until told."*

**Status: registration only. No C5 computation has been run. Nothing below may change once a run begins; a change requires a new registration with a stated reason and both results reported.**

## A. Provenance of the hypothesis

The hypothesis was generated by inspection of C3's context runs (crisis-inclusive window: regime drawdown reduction 63.7% vs vol targeting 57.0%; decision window: regime 63.9% vs 29.7%). It is therefore a post-hoc hypothesis and is tested on the same history that suggested it. That limitation is registered here and must appear in the results report; a pass on this window is evidence of consistency, not an out-of-sample confirmation.

## B. Contestants (three), all through the C3 machinery

Everything not stated here is inherited unchanged from `reports/c3_registration.md` v1 sections B1–B9 (index, cash, window 2010-02-01 → 2026-05-21, monthly rebalance on the first session of each month, signals through t−1, tier mapping, C1 costs 10 bps one-way on NAV-weight turnover, metric definitions) and from v2 for the paired criterion.

- **(a) Regime alone**: C3 contestant (i): cash = clamp(floor + R_full × slope, floor, max); exposure e_r = 1 − cash. Regime input: the as-published series `data/regime_v2_daily.csv` (primary, as in C3) **and** the point-in-time series `data/c2/regime_v2_daily_pit.csv` (reported alongside, as C3 v2's context lines require); the decision is taken on the as-published series to match C3.
- **(b) Volatility targeting alone**: C3 contestant (iv): e_v = min(1.0, 0.10 / σ), σ = annualized standard deviation of the last 60 daily log returns through t−1.
- **(c) Combination, fixed now as the product**: e_c = e_r × e_v. The regime sets the risk budget and volatility targeting scales it; in the pure overlay (tier 4) this is the regime's equity sleeve run at a 10% target. No other combination form (minimum, average, switching) is tested, to avoid multiplicity; if the product fails, the hypothesis fails.
- **Tier mapping** as C3 B7: each contestant's exposure maps into each tier's cash range (rules: cash = floor + (1 − e) × (max − floor); regime: its own formula). The decision is evaluated on tier 4 (pure overlay); tiers 1–3 are reported in full.
- **Benchmark**: buy-and-hold on the index (tier 4: SPY buy-and-hold; tiers 1–3: the tier's floor-cash constant mix), as C3.

## C. Metrics, uncertainty (unchanged from C3)

Maximum drawdown; annualized return; return per unit of volatility; turnover (one-way, annual); drawdown reduction relative to buy-and-hold. Circular block bootstrap of the daily net return series of all strategies jointly: 60-session blocks, 1,000 resamples, seed 20260909, 90% intervals (5th–95th percentiles) on every metric except turnover; paired differences on the same resampled paths.

## D. Decision rule (the paired criterion of C3 v2, applied twice)

The combination **dominates** and may be adopted into sizing only if, on tier 4, both hold against **each** of (a) and (b):
1. the 90% interval of the paired difference (combination − comparator) in after-cost return per unit of volatility lies entirely above zero;
2. the combination's drawdown reduction relative to buy-and-hold is at least the comparator's (point estimates).

Four conditions in total (two per comparator). Any failure = the combination is not adopted; the regime layer keeps its role as decided in the memo's section 4. A pass is reported with the post-hoc caveat of section A and with the point-in-time regime input's result beside it; adoption into live sizing is a separate written decision.
