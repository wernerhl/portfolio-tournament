**Status: registration only. No validation computation has been run. Nothing below may
change once a run begins; a change requires a new registration with a stated reason and both
results reported.** Every candidate stays out of the Business Quality and Trade-Now scores,
and every overlay stays labeled DIAGNOSTIC, until its test passes. None of these ever enters

signal (governing premise).

## A. Provenance of the hypotheses

Each candidate carries a published cross-sectional prior; none was generated by looking at
this system's own data. A pass on the union universe is evidence that the published effect
survives in this universe, in this period, at these costs — not a confirmation of the
published magnitude. The point-in-time option history required for 5.1 and 5.3 must come
from a purchased dataset (item 1.4: ORATS, Cboe DataShop or comparable) or, later, from the
system's own vintage archive once it spans the test window; the archive alone (started
25-Sept-2026) cannot support a run for at least a year.

## B. 5.1 — Options-derived return signals (candidates; union universe only)

Test universe: the full union universe (`data/universe.txt`, 537 names), never the book.
Each candidate is a monthly cross-sectional sort into quintiles; the challenger is the
long-short (top minus bottom quintile) and, separately, the top quintile against the
equal-weight universe. Signals use information through session t−1; positions form on the
first session of each month.

| candidate | prior | construction |
|---|---|---|
| call-minus-put implied-volatility spread | Cremers & Weinbaum (2010) | ATM call IV minus ATM put IV at the front tenor, computed by the system's inversion |
| steepness of the put smirk | Xing, Zhang & Zhao (2010) | OTM put IV (Δ ≈ −0.20) minus ATM call IV at the front tenor |
| changes in call and put implied volatility | An, Ang, Bali & Cakici (2014) | month-over-month change in ATM call IV and in ATM put IV, tested separately |

**Factor neutralization:** each candidate is orthogonalized to size, value, momentum and the
market beta measured over the same window before sorting; the raw sort is reported beside it.
**Costs:** the C1 cost model (10 bps one-way on turnover) plus the options-side cost where a
signal requires an option position (none of the three does; they are stock sorts).

**5.1b — implied-minus-realized gap (Goyal & Saretto 2009):** tested as a predictor of
delta-hedged option returns (sell the straddle, delta-hedge daily, when IV−RV is in the top
quintile; buy when in the bottom), which governs whether selling or buying options on a name
is priced favourably — **not** as a stock-return signal. Costs: option bid-ask at one third of
quoted width plus the hedge's stock turnover at 10 bps.

## C. 5.2 — Event-premium test

For the held names and the top-40 board: does the event-implied move exceed the realized
reaction on average (the event variance premium)? By name and pooled. Statistic: the
exceedance rate (share of releases where |realized reaction| < implied move) with **Wilson
score intervals**, and the mean of (implied − |realized|). Inputs: the implied move from the
vintage on the last session before each release (point-in-time), the realized reaction from
`data/options/earnings_reactions.json`. **The result governs rule 5 of the hedge selector**
(no naked premium selling into an inverted term structure with earnings inside the tenor): the
rule stays as registered unless the pooled exceedance rate's 95% Wilson interval lies entirely
above 0.5 and the premium's paired interval lies above zero, in which case the rule may be
re-registered with a stated reason.

## D. 5.3 — Overlay tests (the hedge selector's DIAGNOSTIC gate)

As specified in `options_layer_design.md` Phase 3 (that document is not on this machine; the
four tests below are the ones the order enumerates), each by the retirement method:

1. **Covered calls** against delta-matched exposure reduction (the same delta cut taken by
   trimming the stock and holding cash).
2. **Collars** against trimming to a 40 percent risk share.
3. **Put-writes on the cash sleeve** against the policy rate (the canonical 3-month bill).
4. **Conditional protection** (puts bought only in the states the lens labels) against the
   regime cash sleeve.

**Method (shared):** point-in-time chains from the purchased history; option costs at one
third of quoted bid-ask width, stock costs at 10 bps; circular block bootstrap of the daily
net return series of all contestants jointly (60-session blocks, 1,000 resamples, seed
20260926, 90% intervals), **paired differences on the same resampled paths** — the statistic
whose discriminating power the regime test v2 established. **Decision:** the overlay is
adopted only if the 90% interval of the paired difference (overlay − comparator) in after-cost
return per unit of volatility lies entirely above zero and its drawdown reduction is at least
the comparator's. **The DIAGNOSTIC label on the hedge selector lifts only on a pass**, and
adoption into any execution path is a separate written decision by Werner Hernani-Limarino.
